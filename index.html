Methodology & labels

Exact construction from the paper (2023 sample, n = 14,912):
① HFRS = Σ wᵢ·zᵢ: weighted sum of the 8 standardized indicators, weights wᵢ from PCA (Table 7).
② HFRS(0–100) = 100 × (HFRS − min) / (max − min): min–max rescaled over the 2023 sample.
Sliders show each indicator in its original units (dollars, ratios, or category) and walk the 2023 percentile range; internally each value is mapped back to its standardized (z) form via the monotonic empirical transform, so the score stays exact. The percentile beside each value is its rank in the 2023 sample. Contribution chips show each indicator's points on the 0–100 scale.

Label under the score = UMAP type family & clustering segment, each the nearest match to the current slider profile (smallest Euclidean distance in the 8-indicator z-space to the group centroids). Type families (ratio signature): D Debt-driven, DS Debt-driven+Saver, E Multi-earner, F Financial-asset & liquidity-led, B Balanced, T Thin-wealth, W Wealth-anchored. Clusters (wealth level): C1 low-wealth/renters, C2 mid-wealth/mortgaged, C3 high-wealth/outright owners. The two are orthogonal lenses: a ratio signature within a wealth tier.
